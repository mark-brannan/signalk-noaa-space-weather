What you're looking at is a snapshot: real NOAA data, captured once and saved into this page, so it stays fast and can't break. The boat is a stand-in too — on the water the plugin takes your own position from Signal K, polls NOAA continuously, publishes these numbers, raises notifications when a storm arrives, and overlays this map on your own charts.

Like it? Run it on your own boat — it installs from the Signal K app store.
